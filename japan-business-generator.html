Question 6 of 6

Which Japan megatrend excites you most?

Japan is in the middle of several massive structural shifts. Aligning with a trend you believe in means the market works with you, not against you.

👴
Aging Population & Silver Economy
29% of Japan is 65+ — the world's most aged society. Demand for care, convenience, tech assistance, and health services is catastrophically underserved and government-subsidized.
🗼
Tourism Boom & Unique Experiences
Record 31.9M tourists in 2023 spending ¥5.3 trillion. The weak yen is driving unprecedented inbound spending. Tourists want authenticity over mass attractions.
🖥️
Digital Transformation (DX)
Japan ranks 29th globally in digital competitiveness despite being a tech nation. Fax machines, paper forms, and hanko stamps dominate SMEs — massive B2B opportunity with government subsidies.
🌾
Rural Revitalization & Akiya
9M+ vacant homes. Shrinking towns offering land and grants to attract entrepreneurs. Digital nomad visas, remote work culture, and ¥1M+ municipal relocation subsidies open new frontiers.
🎋
Japanese Culture Goes Global
Anime market exceeded $30B globally in 2023. J-beauty, Japanese food, craftsmanship, and wellness concepts have unprecedented global demand. Weak yen makes sourcing from Japan highly competitive.
